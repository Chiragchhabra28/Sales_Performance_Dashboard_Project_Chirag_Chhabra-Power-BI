Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to effectively monitor and evaluate their sales performance due to scattered lead data and lack of centralized reporting. Key challenges include:

Inability to track overall lead conversion performance

Difficulty identifying high-performing and underperforming sales counsellors

Limited visibility into revenue contribution from different lead sources

Lack of clarity in sales funnel drop-off points

Challenges in analyzing call outcomes and engagement effectiveness

Absence of real-time insights for strategic decision-making

Without proper analysis, businesses risk losing potential customers, inefficient marketing spend, and reduced sales productivity.

💡 Proposed Solution

To address these challenges, an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard was developed using Power BI. The dashboard consolidates sales and lead data into a centralized platform, providing clear and actionable insights.

The solution enables stakeholders to:

✔ Monitor overall sales performance through real-time KPIs
✔ Analyze lead source effectiveness and marketing ROI
✔ Evaluate counsellor performance using conversion and revenue metrics
✔ Track sales funnel progression and identify drop-off stages
✔ Examine call outcomes to improve engagement strategies
✔ Visualize lead trends across different time periods
